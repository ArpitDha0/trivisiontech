Overview

The AP50 Vision MU-RTK GNSS receiver integrates advanced GNSS, IMU, and dual-camera technologies. The front camera enables video-photogrammetry for superior user experience, while the bottom camera features AR visual navigation and stakeout capabilities. Operators can cut workload by half and complete oblique imagery surveys efficiently. Fully compatible with leading 3D modeling software.

Key Features

  • Up to 120° IMU tilt compensation
  • Visual surveying & AR stakeout
  • Dual cameras (front 2MP + bottom 5MP)
  • 1408 channels – All-constellation tracking
  • Built-in 2W UHF Tx/Rx radio (15 km range)
  • 9000 mAh battery – All-day operation

Solution & Results

The AP50 Vision revolutionizes traditional surveying by combining centimeter-level RTK accuracy with real-time visual guidance. Surveyors can now stake out points accurately even in obstructed environments using AR navigation. The system supports direct CAD import, 3D coordinate staking, and seamless data transfer to modeling software – reducing field time by up to 50% and eliminating human error in complex projects.
