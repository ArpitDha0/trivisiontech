Overview

The AP10 GNSS is a powerful and cost-effective professional-grade GNSS receiver featuring a built-in 2W UHF modem (8–15 km working range). It is perfect for base station or rover applications. With up to 120° tilt IMU, the built-in tilt sensor increases surveying efficiency by up to 30%. It delivers highly productive RTK performance for surveying and stakeout in any topographic, mapping, or construction site application.

Key Product Features

  • 1408 channels – All constellation & frequency tracking
  • Up to 120° IMU tilt compensation (accuracy <2cm within 60°)
  • Built-in 2W UHF Tx/Rx radio – 8–15 km range
  • IP67 dust & waterproof + rugged magnesium alloy body
  • 9000 mAh high-capacity battery – all-day operation
  • Supports NTRIP, RTCM 3.x, CMR – base/rover flexible
  • Web UI management + firmware upgrade via WiFi
