Overview

The Apeks CS3i is a military-grade rugged data controller that combines industrial toughness with Android 11 performance. Featuring an ultra-bright 5" sunlight-readable touchscreen, physical QWERTY keypad, 9000mAh hot-swappable battery, and full 4G/WiFi/Bluetooth connectivity, the CS3i is engineered to excel in the harshest surveying conditions — from -40°C arctic jobs to +75°C desert heat.

  • 5" ultra-bright 720×1440p sunlight-readable capacitive touchscreen

  • 9000mAh high-capacity hot-swappable battery – 18h+ field work
